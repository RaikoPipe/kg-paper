\subsection{Ethical and/or legal aspects of the project}

\subsubsection{General ethical aspects}
The project uses \acp{LLM} on planning documents. Confidential industrial documents are processed only under data-use agreements and preferably with open-weight models on in-house hardware; no confidential content is sent to external services without the consent of the data owner. Generative AI was used to support the drafting of this proposal; all content was checked and is the responsibility of the applicants. \todo{finalise the AI-use statement required by form 54.01}

\subsubsection{Descriptions of proposed investigations on humans, human materials or identifiable data}
The planner study in \wpref{5} involves adult professional volunteers who rate answers of an assistance system. No health data or other special categories of personal data are collected. Participants give informed written consent, can withdraw at any time, and ratings are stored pseudonymised in accordance with the GDPR. The study protocol will be submitted to the ethics committee of the responsible university if required by its rules. \todo{check ethics committee requirements at OVGU and TU Dresden}
